Support · One human, no ticket portal

The course is stuck. Let’s be specific.

Email me@demetria.xyz with the lesson, what you expected, what happened, and whether you were in the browser or the app. A screenshot helps. A paragraph of feelings helps less.

Common ones

An exercise will not pass

Read the first failing test, not the last. The tests are literal. If the lesson is an Xcode lab, the on-device engine cannot check it; open the tests and the starting code, then use Xcode on a Mac.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored in your browser or, if you signed in, in your Asteria account. Clearing site data clears the local copy. If you signed in and it is still missing, email me with the account name and the lesson you last finished.

The Mac app is not in the App Store yet

Correct. It is in review. The free web course is available now, and the 1.0 Mac app still exists for older systems. The 2.0 app will be linked from asteria.technology/swift when it is out.

iCloud is not syncing in the app

Open Settings in the app and read the iCloud status. If it says “on this device”, the build has no entitlement or iCloud is unavailable. Sign in to iCloud in System Settings and relaunch. The web course does not use iCloud; it uses your Asteria account if you sign in.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no Asteria analytics. The course uses the runner to check code; it is not a profiling service.
