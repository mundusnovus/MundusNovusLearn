Swift: Zero to Hero for Mac

The whole course in a native Mac app: all 161 lessons, from your first line of Swift to shipping apps for iPhone and Mac. Your code compiles and runs with the Swift compiler on your own Mac, so it never leaves your computer and the course works offline.

Download for Mac (1.6 MB) Version 1.0.0 · signed and notarized by Apple

Getting started

  1. Open the download and drag Swift Zero to Hero to your Applications folder.
  2. The exercises need Apple's free developer tools. If your Mac does not have them yet, the app offers to install the Command Line Tools for you. Xcode works too.
  3. Your progress and your code are saved on your Mac.

Coming to the Mac App Store. The same app will also be on the Mac App Store, where your purchase supports Asteria Technology. The course stays free here and on the website.
