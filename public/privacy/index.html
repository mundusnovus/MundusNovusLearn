Privacy

We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising. There is no “personalised learning” that is really a profiling pipeline.

Swift: Zero to Hero for iPhone, iPad and Mac

The app collects no analytics. Your lessons, code, checklist ticks, streaks and badges are stored on your device and, when iCloud is available, in your own private CloudKit database. Your code is checked on the device on iPhone and iPad; on the Mac it compiles with your own Swift compiler. Nothing is sent to an Asteria code-checking server.

Apple Intelligence features run on the device. Private Cloud Compute is compiled in but disabled until Apple grants the managed entitlement. If it is enabled in a future build, requests go to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute under Apple’s terms, not to an Asteria server.

The website

You can read every lesson without an account. Running code and keeping progress across devices needs an Asteria account, which you sign in to with Asteria Social.

  • What we keep: your account name, the lessons you finish, and the code that passed each lesson, so you can come back to it.
  • Running code: when you press Run on the website, your code is sent to our server, compiled, run in a sandbox and then deleted. We count runs per hour to keep the service fair.
  • Without an account: your progress is kept in your own browser.
  • Cookies: one cookie keeps you signed in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Other Asteria apps

Browse, Keys, Timeclock, Food and Mood, Box and Asteria Search each have their own data practices. The short version is the same: no Asteria account, no analytics, no ads, and your work stays on your device or in your private iCloud. The full policy is at asteria.technology/privacy.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it at any time, and we will. Write to us through Asteria support.
